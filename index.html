About Elim School

An Oasis of Knowledge, Character & Inspiration in Kitwe

Elim School is a premier, multi-racial, co-educational private day school situated on Plot 21 Pamo Avenue in the heart of Parklands, Kitwe, Zambia.

The name "Elim" originates from the historic oasis characterized by twelve fresh water springs and seventy sheltering palm trees—a tranquil haven of rest, revival, and fruitful growth. In the very same spirit, Elim School was established in 1998 to be a nurturing haven where every child is loved, mentored, and challenged to achieve their highest intellectual and ethical potential.

Over the past quarter-century, we have earned a distinguished reputation on the Copperbelt for cultivating well-rounded learners who transition confidently into secondary schools with exemplary discipline and academic vigor.

Academic Excellence

Mastery of reading, writing, mathematical thinking, and practical scientific inquiry.

Moral Integrity

Instilling honesty, courtesy, mutual respect, and Christian moral principles.

Multicultural Community

A welcoming, inclusive atmosphere celebrating diversity and cultural unity.

Creative & Physical Growth

Nurturing artistic self-expression, music, athletics, and teamwork outside the classroom.

Academics & Curriculum

Comprehensive Education for Every Stage

Following the official Zambian Ministry of Education syllabus enriched with modern early learning frameworks to develop critical thinkers and confident communicators.

Ages 6 Months – 5 Years

Early Childhood Education & Day Care

Our ECE division comprises Day-Care (Babies), Baby Class, Nursery, Pre-School, and Reception. We emphasize safe, loving care and play-based learning that awakens curiosity, oral vocabulary, fine motor control, and foundational numeracy.

Day-Care (Infant & Toddler Care) Jolly Phonics & Early Literacy Number Readiness & Shapes Sensory & Sand/Water Play Social Etiquette & Self-Care Nursery Rhymes & Music
Grades 1 through 4

Lower Primary: Building Strong Foundations

Lower Primary transitions learners into structured academic disciplines. With small class sizes, our teachers give focused attention to reading fluency, mathematical comprehension, and handwriting, while cultivating a genuine love for learning.

English Language & Reading Mathematics & Numeracy Integrated Science Social Studies Creative & Technology Studies (CTS) Zambian Language Studies
Grades 5, 6 & 7 (ECZ Exam Class)

Upper Primary & Grade 7 Exam Excellence

Upper Primary sharpens students' critical reasoning, scientific experimentation, and analytical writing. In Grade 7, students undergo intensive preparation for the Zambian National Examinations Council (ECZ) composite exams with consistently outstanding pass rates.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, fees, daily schedules, and student life at Elim School.

Elim School caters to children from infancy to completion of primary education: Day-Care (Babies from 6 months), Baby Class / Nursery (ages 2–3), Pre-School (ages 3–4), Reception (ages 4–5), and Primary Grades 1 through 7 (ages 6 to 13+).
Our administrative office operates Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Normal classroom hours run from 7:45 AM, with early childhood ending at 12:30 PM and primary classes concluding between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM depending on the grade and co-curricular schedule.
Prospective learners undergo a friendly, age-appropriate assessment designed to identify their foundational literacy, numeracy, and developmental milestones. This ensures they are placed in the class that best aligns with their learning pace and potential. An assessment fee of K200.00 is required upon application.
Official Elim School uniforms (day uniform and sportswear) are available directly on campus at our school office. In addition, parents supply one (1) ream of printing paper per year and two (2) rolls of tissue per term. Specific exercise book checklists are provided upon admission.
School fees are due on or before the opening day of each term. Payments can be deposited directly into our official school bank account, paid via authorized direct bank transfer, or settled at the school bursar's office. PTA contributions (K200 per term) are deposited directly into the designated PTA account.
Yes, transfers are welcome subject to space availability. Transfer applicants must submit their previous school's report card, a clearance or transfer letter, and undergo our standard placement assessment.
